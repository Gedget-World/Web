# Shipping, Cancellation, Exchange, Returns and Cash on Delivery

## Cash on Delivery (COD) Policy

- For Cash on Delivery orders, **20% of the final amount** is to be paid in advance for orders **below Rs. 10,000**, and **15% of the final amount** is to be paid in advance for orders **above Rs. 10,000**.
- Cash on Delivery can only take place on an order value of **Rs. 549 and above**.
- For cash on delivery orders, a **minimum order of Rs. 549** is required and **20% of the final amount** is to be paid in advance.

### Refusal / Failed Delivery (Orders above Rs. 10,000)

If you refuse to accept the delivery of the order, or the order is returned because of:

- Wrong address
- Unanswered calls
- No response from the customer

…there will be a **50% penalty** deducted from the advance amount before the refund is processed. Refunds will be credited to the original payment method within **7–15 days** after the return of the shipment.

### Refusal / Failed Delivery (Orders below Rs. 10,000)

For orders below Rs. 10,000, **there will be no refund** if they are returned to us.

### Cancellation After Advance Payment

If you choose to cancel the order after making the advance payment, there will be a **50% penalty** deducted from the paid advance amount before the refund is processed.

## Shipping / Delivery Terms and Conditions

### Products

- We dispatch orders **within the same business day**.
- A **tracking link** is provided via email / WhatsApp after order confirmation / completion.
- We try hard and make the best of our efforts to deliver your ordered products in excellent condition and in the fastest possible time.
- All shipments usually get delivered within **5–10 working days** from the shipment date (except during natural calamities, pandemics, and festivals). Non-metro cities may take longer than usual.
- **We do not ship orders on Sundays.** Non-working days are not counted.
- We keep you notified through email / WhatsApp notifications, so please keep checking your email / WhatsApp.

## Return / Refund / Repair / Cancellation

- Once your order is delivered, there are **absolutely no returns / refunds**.
- The delivered product (covered under Manufacturer Warranty) is guaranteed to be exactly as described and in working condition.
- If your delivered product is **not as described** or **not in working condition**, email us at **GADGETSKABILA@gmail.com** within **24 hours of delivery** with the required proof.
- In such cases, please record an **unboxing / unpacking video** of your order, including the packaging label, to establish proof. A replacement will be issued only after validating the necessary information. A reverse pickup will be arranged by us for a smooth return process (if applicable).

### Imported Products

For imported products, we **do not provide any replacement policy**. Only repair is possible in the following conditions:

- Manufacturing issues, or
- Dead on Arrival (DOA)

We do **not** provide any additional warranty. Always check with chat support for better information.

### Refund on Cancellation

- Any refund requested by the customer will attract a **penalty** under the refund rules / policy.
- The Company provides refund / replacement only in case of **faulty and damaged condition**, after investigation.

### Refund Processing

Refunds for cancelled orders will be processed only through the **same mode of payment** used during the transaction (i.e., to the same account).

| Payment Mode             | Refund Processing Time                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Credit Card / Debit Card | Approximately **8–10 business days** (as per bank's standard time frame) |
| COD / Cheque / DD        | **15–20 working days**                                                   |

- Cheques will be made as per the **billing name** provided.
- For non-delivered items, refunds will be processed only on confirmation that the product was not delivered to you and you choose to take a refund and are not interested in any other product.
