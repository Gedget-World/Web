## 3. Return Policy

### 3.1 Return Window

- Products may be returned within **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "7 days"]`** from the Delivery Date ("Return Window").
- Return requests made after the Return Window will **not** be accepted unless the product is covered under warranty or received in a defective/damaged condition.

### 3.2 Return Eligibility

A product is eligible for return only if:

- It is within the Return Window.
- It is **unused, unworn, and in its original condition**.
- It is in its **original packaging** with all tags, labels, manuals, accessories, free gifts, and documentation intact.
- It is **not** a Non-Returnable Item (see Section 5).
- The product seal, shrink wrap, or tamper-proof seal is **unbroken** (for electronics, software, and sealed items).
- You provide a valid **reason for return** along with supporting photos/videos (if applicable).

6. Our team will review your request and respond within **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "24-48 hours"]`**.
7. If approved, you will receive return instructions including a **pickup schedule** or **self-ship instructions**.

- Products that have been **altered, modified, or repaired** by you or an unauthorized third party after delivery.

- Your return request has been **approved** and the product has been received and **passed quality inspection** at our warehouse.
- Your order has been **cancelled** (by you or by us) before or after dispatch.
- You received a **defective, damaged, or wrong** product and opted for a refund instead of replacement.
- A product you ordered is **out of stock** and cannot be fulfilled.

- **Restocking Fee:** Up to `⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "15%"]` of the product price may be deducted if the product is returned in a condition that is not in original/resaleable state.
- **Reverse Shipping Charges:** If you refused delivery or initiated a change-of-mind return, reverse logistics charges of up to ₹`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "100-200"]` may be deducted from the refund.
- **Free Gift/Promotional Item Value:** If the order included a free gift or promotional offer, the value of the gift may be deducted from the refund if the gift is not returned along with the product.

### 7.5 Refund for Partial Orders

- If you cancel or return only some items from a multi-item order, the refund will be calculated based on the price of the returned/cancelled items.
- If a coupon or discount was applied to the order, the refund will be adjusted proportionally based on the discount allocated to the returned item(s).
