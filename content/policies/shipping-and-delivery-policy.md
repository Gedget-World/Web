## 5. Order Tracking

### 5.1 How to Track Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:

- A **dispatch confirmation email and/or SMS** containing your tracking number and courier partner details.
- A **tracking link** to monitor the real-time status of your shipment.

You can also track your order by:

1. Logging into your **Gadgets Kabila account** → **"My Orders"** → Select the order → **"Track Order"**.
2. Visiting the courier partner's website and entering your tracking number.
3. Contacting our customer support with your order number.

### 5.2 Tracking Updates

Typical tracking statuses include:

- **Order Confirmed** — Your order has been received and payment verified.
- **Processing** — Your order is being packed and prepared for dispatch.
- **Dispatched/Shipped** — Your order has been picked up by the courier partner.
- **In Transit** — Your package is on its way to your delivery address.
- **Out for Delivery** — Your package is with the delivery agent and will be delivered today.
- **Delivered** — Your package has been successfully delivered.
- **Failed Delivery Attempt** — The delivery agent was unable to deliver (see Section 6.3).

### 5.3 No Tracking Updates

If your tracking status has not updated for more than **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "48-72 hours"]`**, please contact our customer support. We will coordinate with the logistics partner to investigate and provide an update.

## 6. Delivery Process

### 6.1 Delivery Attempts

- Our logistics partners will make up to **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "2-3"]` delivery attempts** at no additional charge.
- If delivery fails on all attempts (due to incorrect address, recipient unavailable, premises closed, etc.), the package will be **returned to our warehouse** (Return to Origin — RTO).
- In case of RTO, a refund will be processed minus applicable **reverse shipping charges** as per our Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy.

### 6.2 Delivery Verification

- The delivery agent may request **identity verification** (government-issued photo ID) for high-value orders.
- For **Cash on Delivery (COD)** orders, the exact payment amount must be paid to the delivery agent at the time of delivery. Our delivery agents do **not carry change**.
- You or your authorized representative at the delivery address may receive the order on your behalf.

### 6.3 Failed Delivery

If the delivery agent is unable to deliver your order, they will:

- Attempt to contact you at the phone number provided on your order.
- Leave a notification about the failed attempt (via SMS/call).
- Reattempt delivery on the next business day.

To avoid failed deliveries, please ensure:

- Your delivery address is **accurate and complete** (including flat/house number, landmark, and PIN code).
- Your **phone number** is reachable during delivery hours.
- Someone is available at the delivery address to receive the order.

### 6.4 Delivery Hours

Deliveries are generally made between **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "9:00 AM and 8:00 PM"]`**, Monday through Saturday. Delivery times may vary based on the logistics partner and your location.

### 6.5 Contactless / Safe Delivery

We may offer contactless delivery options in compliance with health and safety guidelines. If applicable, the delivery agent will place the package at your doorstep and notify you.

## 9. Delays & Force Majeure

### 9.1 Delivery Delays

While we strive to deliver within estimated timelines, delays may occur due to:

- High demand during **sales, festivals, and promotional events** (e.g., Diwali, Republic Day Sale, etc.).
- **Adverse weather conditions** (floods, cyclones, heavy rain, snowfall, etc.).
- **Natural disasters** (earthquakes, landslides, etc.).
- **Public holidays** and regional festivals.
- **Government-imposed restrictions**, curfews, or lockdowns.
- **Logistics partner operational issues** (vehicle breakdowns, staff shortages, route disruptions).
- **Customs clearance delays** (for international orders).
- **Incorrect or incomplete delivery address** provided by you.
- **Address inaccessible** to the delivery agent.

### 9.2 Communication About Delays

- If we anticipate a significant delay (more than `⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "3 business days"]` beyond the estimated delivery date), we will notify you via **email and/or SMS** with an updated estimated delivery date.
- You may choose to **wait for delivery** or **cancel the order for a full refund** if the delay is unacceptable.

### 9.3 Force Majeure

Gadgets Kabila shall not be held liable for any delay or failure in delivery caused by events beyond our reasonable control (**Force Majeure**), including but not limited to:

- Natural disasters (floods, earthquakes, cyclones, droughts)
- Epidemics, pandemics, and public health emergencies
- War, terrorism, civil unrest, riots, and strikes
- Government actions, laws, regulations, embargoes, or sanctions
- Failures of telecommunications, internet, or power supply
- Failures of third-party logistics or courier services

In the event of a Force Majeure, delivery obligations shall be suspended for the duration of the event. We will resume delivery as soon as reasonably possible and keep you informed.

## 10. Lost, Damaged, or Stolen Shipments

### 10.1 Lost Shipments

If your order is not delivered within the estimated timeline and tracking shows no updates for more than **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "7 days"]`** after the estimated delivery date:

1. Contact our customer support with your order number.
2. We will initiate an investigation with the logistics partner.
3. If the shipment is confirmed lost, we will offer a **full refund** or **re-shipment** at no additional cost (subject to product availability).
4. Resolution timeline: Within `⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "7-10 business days"]` from the date the investigation is initiated.

### 10.2 Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives with visible damage to the packaging or the product:

1. **Do not discard** the packaging material.
2. **Document the damage** with clear photos/videos of the outer packaging, inner packaging, and the damaged product.
3. **Report within 48 hours** of delivery by initiating a return request or contacting customer support.
4. We will arrange a **free reverse pickup** and provide a **replacement or full refund** as per our Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy.

**Tip:** We recommend **recording an unboxing video** for every order, especially for high-value or fragile items. This serves as evidence in case of damage claims.

### 10.3 Stolen Shipments (Marked Delivered But Not Received)

If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you have not received the package:

1. Check with **neighbors, family members, building security, or reception** who may have accepted the delivery on your behalf.
2. Contact our customer support within **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "48 hours"]`** of the delivery status update.
3. We will coordinate with the logistics partner to investigate (delivery agent GPS data, delivery photo proof, etc.).
4. Based on the investigation outcome, we will offer a **refund or re-shipment** as appropriate.
