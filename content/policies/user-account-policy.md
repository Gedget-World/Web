## 4. Account Security

### 4.1 Your Responsibilities

You are solely responsible for maintaining the security of your account. You agree to:

- **Create a strong password:** Use a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Avoid easily guessable passwords (e.g., "123456," "password," your name, or birthdate).
- **Keep credentials confidential:** Do not share your password, OTP, or login details with anyone.
- **Use unique credentials:** Do not reuse passwords from other websites or services.
- **Secure your devices:** Ensure that devices used to access your account are protected with screen locks, antivirus software, and updated operating systems.
- **Log out after use:** Always log out from your account, especially when using shared or public devices.
- **Monitor account activity:** Regularly review your account activity, order history, and profile information for unauthorized changes.

### 4.2 Unauthorized Access

If you suspect or become aware of:

- Unauthorized access to your account.
- Unauthorized transactions or orders.
- Compromise of your password or credentials.
- Any other security breach.

You must **immediately**:

3. If linked payment methods may be compromised, notify your bank or card issuer.

### 4.3 Our Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your account:

- **Encrypted Data Transmission:** All data is transmitted over secure HTTPS (SSL/TLS) connections.
- **Password Hashing:** Passwords are stored using strong cryptographic hashing algorithms. We do not store passwords in plain text.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** `⚠️ [UPDATE: If 2FA is available, state: "We offer optional two-factor authentication via SMS OTP or authenticator app for added security." If not, state: "Two-factor authentication is planned for future implementation."]`

- **Suspicious Activity Detection:** Automated monitoring for unusual login patterns, multiple failed login attempts, and potential account takeover attempts.

### 4.4 Liability for Unauthorized Activity

- Gadgets Kabila shall **not be liable** for any loss or damage arising from unauthorized access to your account due to your failure to maintain the confidentiality of your credentials.
- If unauthorized activity occurs due to a security breach on our systems (not attributable to your negligence), we will work with you to mitigate the impact and restore your account.
