## 1. What Are Cookies?

**Cookies** are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to make websites work efficiently, enhance user experience, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies can be:

- **First-Party Cookies:** Set by the website you are visiting (Gadgets Kabila).
- **Third-Party Cookies:** Set by external services integrated into our website (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook, advertising networks).

Cookies can also be classified by their duration:

- **Session Cookies:** Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
- **Persistent Cookies:** Remain on your device for a set period or until you manually delete them.

## 3. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Essential / Strictly Necessary

To enable core website functionality that you cannot opt out of:

- **Authentication:** Keeping you logged in during your session.
- **Security:** Protecting against fraud, unauthorized access, and malicious activity (e.g., CSRF tokens).
- **Shopping Cart:** Remembering items in your cart as you browse.
- **Session Management:** Maintaining your session state across page loads.
- **Load Balancing:** Distributing traffic to ensure website performance.
- **Cookie Consent:** Remembering your cookie preferences.

### 3.2 Functional / Preference

To remember your choices and provide enhanced features:

- **Language & Region:** Remembering your preferred language and country/region.
- **Display Preferences:** Remembering theme settings (light/dark mode), font size, etc.
- **Form Data:** Remembering information you entered in forms to avoid re-entry.
- **Recently Viewed Products:** Showing your browsing history for convenience.

### 3.3 Analytics / Performance

To understand how visitors use our website and improve performance:

- **Traffic Analysis:** Measuring the number of visitors, page views, bounce rate, and session duration.
- **User Behavior:** Understanding how users navigate the site, which pages are popular, and where users drop off.
- **Error Tracking:** Identifying and diagnosing technical issues.
- **A/B Testing:** Testing different versions of pages or features to optimize user experience.

### 3.4 Advertising / Targeting / Marketing

To deliver personalized advertisements and measure their effectiveness:

- **Interest-Based Advertising:** Showing you ads relevant to your interests based on your browsing behavior.
- **Retargeting / Remarketing:** Showing you ads for products you previously viewed on our site when you browse other websites.
- **Conversion Tracking:** Measuring whether you completed a purchase after clicking on an ad.
- **Ad Frequency Capping:** Limiting how often you see the same ad.
- **Cross-Device Tracking:** Linking your activity across devices for consistent ad targeting.

### 3.5 Social Media

To enable social media features and integrations:

- **Social Sharing:** Allowing you to share products or content on social media platforms.
- **Social Login:** Enabling login via Facebook, Google, or other social accounts.
- **Embedded Content:** Displaying embedded social media posts or videos.
