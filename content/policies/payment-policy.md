## 3. EMI (Equated Monthly Installments)

### 3.1 Availability

- EMI options are available on select **credit cards** from participating banks.
- EMI is available on products with a minimum price of ₹`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "3,000"]` or as determined by the issuing bank.
- EMI tenures typically range from **3 to 24 months**, depending on the bank and card type.

### 3.2 Types of EMI

| **EMI Type**     | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Standard EMI** | Total amount is divided into monthly installments with applicable **interest charged by your bank**.                                                                                                               |
| **No-Cost EMI**  | Total amount is divided into monthly installments with **no additional interest**. The discount/cashback equivalent to the interest is provided by Gadgets Kabila or the brand. Available on select products only. |

### 3.3 How EMI Works

1. Select your product and proceed to checkout.
2. Choose **"EMI"** as the payment method.
3. Select your **bank** and preferred **EMI tenure**.
4. The EMI amount per month, total interest (if any), and total payable amount will be displayed.
5. Complete the payment using your credit card.
6. Your bank will convert the transaction to EMI and it will reflect on your next credit card statement.

### 3.4 EMI Terms & Conditions

- EMI is a facility provided by your **card-issuing bank**, not by Gadgets Kabila. All EMI terms, interest rates, and processing fees are determined by your bank.
- **Pre-closure or foreclosure** of EMI is subject to your bank's policies and may attract additional charges.
- In case of a **return/refund** on an EMI order, the bank will reverse the EMI and process the refund. EMI cancellation and interest reversal timelines depend on your bank (typically 7-15 business days). Any interest already charged will be reversed by the bank.
- Gadgets Kabila is **not responsible** for any interest, charges, or fees applied by your bank in connection with EMI.

### 4.2 Data Security

We take the security of your financial information very seriously:

- **Encryption:** All payment data is transmitted over a **256-bit SSL/TLS encrypted** connection (HTTPS).
- **No Storage of Card Details:** We do **not** store your complete credit card number, CVV, or PIN on our servers. All sensitive payment information is handled directly by our PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway partners.
- **Tokenization:** Where supported, card details are securely tokenized as per **RBI guidelines on card-on-file tokenization** (effective from October 2022). Tokenized cards allow you to make repeat purchases without re-entering card details, while ensuring your actual card data is never stored by us.
- **3D Secure Authentication:** All card transactions are protected by **3D Secure (3DS) authentication** (Verified by Visa, MasterCard SecureCode, RuPay PaySecure) for an additional layer of security.

- **Velocity checks** to detect unusual transaction patterns.

- Suspicious accounts may be **flagged, suspended, or terminated**.

If you suspect unauthorized use of your payment method on our Platform, please contact us immediately at `⚠️ [UPDATE: Enter support email]` and notify your bank.

## 5. Failed, Pending & Declined Transactions

### 5.1 Failed Transactions

A transaction may fail due to:

- Insufficient funds in your account.
- Incorrect card details, CVV, or OTP.
- Card expired or blocked.
- Bank server downtime or technical issues.
- Transaction limits exceeded (daily/per-transaction limit set by your bank).
- 3D Secure authentication failure.
- Payment gateway technical issues.

**What happens when a transaction fails:**

- Your order will **not** be placed.
- If the amount was debited from your account but the order was not placed, the amount will be **automatically refunded** to your original payment method within **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "5-7 business days"]`**.
- If you do not receive the refund within the stated period, please contact our customer support with your **order number** (if generated) and **transaction reference/UTR number**.

### 5.2 Pending Transactions

Occasionally, a transaction may remain in a **"pending"** state due to:

- Bank processing delays.
- Payment gateway verification in progress.
- Network timeout during payment.

**For pending transactions:**

- Do **not** retry the payment immediately to avoid duplicate charges.
- Wait for **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "30 minutes to 2 hours"]`** for the status to update.
- If the transaction remains pending, the amount will be automatically released by your bank within **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "3-7 business days"]`**.
- Contact our customer support if you need assistance.

### 5.3 Duplicate Payments

If you are charged **twice** for the same order due to a system error or network issue:

- Contact our customer support immediately with your order number and bank statement showing the duplicate charge.
- The duplicate amount will be refunded within **`⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "5-7 business days"]`** after verification.

### 5.4 Declined Transactions

Your payment may be declined by your bank due to:

- Suspected fraudulent activity on your account.
- International transaction restrictions.
- Insufficient credit limit.
- Bank-specific security policies.

**If your transaction is declined:**

- Try a **different payment method**.
- Contact your **bank** to authorize the transaction or lift any restrictions.
- Ensure your card is **enabled for online/e-commerce transactions**.
- Contact our customer support if the issue persists.
