## 2. Price Display & Transparency

### 2.1 All-Inclusive Pricing

**Our prices are displayed inclusive of all applicable taxes**, including:

- **Goods and Services Tax (GST)** — at the applicable rate based on the product category.
- **Integrated GST (IGST)** — for inter-state transactions (included in displayed price).
- **Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST)** — for intra-state transactions (included in displayed price).

**What you see is what you pay** (excluding shipping charges and any applicable additional fees disclosed at checkout).

### 2.2 MRP (Maximum Retail Price)

- Where applicable, the **MRP (Maximum Retail Price)** as declared by the manufacturer is displayed on the product page.
- Our selling price will **never exceed** the MRP declared on the product packaging.
- If our selling price is lower than the MRP, the **discount amount or percentage** will be clearly displayed.

### 2.3 Price Breakdown

On the product page and during checkout, you will see:

| **Component**        | **Details**                                                      |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Selling Price**    | The price at which we offer the product (inclusive of GST)       |
| **MRP**              | Maximum Retail Price declared by manufacturer (where applicable) |
| **Discount**         | Percentage or amount saved compared to MRP                       |
| **Shipping Charges** | Displayed separately at checkout (see Section 4)                 |
| **Total Amount**     | Final amount payable                                             |

### 2.4 Tax Breakup on Invoice

Your tax invoice will clearly show:

- **Taxable Value** (Price excluding GST)
- **GST Rate** applicable to the product
- **CGST Amount** (for intra-state)
- **SGST Amount** (for intra-state)
- **IGST Amount** (for inter-state)
- **Total Tax Amount**
- **Grand Total** (Taxable Value + Total Tax)

## 6. Pricing Errors

### 6.1 Our Commitment to Accuracy

We make every reasonable effort to ensure that all prices displayed on the Platform are accurate and up-to-date. However, despite our best efforts, pricing errors may occasionally occur due to:

- Technical or system glitches
- Human error during data entry
- Supplier or manufacturer price changes not immediately updated
- Website display issues

### 6.2 Right to Correct Errors

Gadgets Kabila reserves the right to **correct any pricing errors** at any time, even after an order has been placed.

### 6.3 Handling of Pricing Errors

**If we discover a pricing error before dispatch:**

1. **Significant Underpricing:** If a product is listed at a price significantly below its actual value due to an error:
   - We will **contact you** via email or phone to inform you of the correct price.
   - You may choose to:
     - **Proceed with the order** at the correct price.
     - **Cancel the order** for a full refund.
   - If we cannot reach you or do not receive a response within `⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "48 hours"]`, we may **cancel the order** and process a full refund.

2. **Overpricing:** If you were charged more than the correct price:
   - We will **refund the difference** to your original payment method.
   - The refund will be processed within `⚠️ [UPDATE: e.g., "5-7 business days"]`.

**If we discover a pricing error after dispatch:**

- For **underpriced items**: We may contact you to discuss the matter. We will not demand additional payment if the product is already in transit or delivered, unless the error was caused by fraud or manipulation on your part.
- For **overpriced items**: We will issue a refund for the difference.

### 6.4 Obvious Errors

In cases of **obvious pricing errors** (e.g., a ₹50,000 laptop listed at ₹500), we reserve the right to:

- Cancel the order without prior notice.
- Refuse to honor the incorrect price.
- Contact you to offer the product at the correct price.

We appreciate your understanding that such errors are unintentional and that honoring them would cause significant financial harm.

### 6.5 Your Rights

- You will **not be charged** more than the price confirmed at the time of order placement, except to correct an error where you agree to the correct price.
- If we cancel your order due to a pricing error, you will receive a **full refund** of any amount paid.
- You will be informed of any pricing error-related cancellation via email.

## 12. Legal Compliance

### 12.1 Consumer Protection Laws

Our pricing practices comply with:

- **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** — prohibition of unfair trade practices, including misleading pricing.
- **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** — requirement for transparent pricing and disclosure of all charges.
- **Legal Metrology Act, 2009** and **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011** — requirements for MRP declaration on packaged goods.
- **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)** regulations where applicable.

### 12.2 Anti-Profiteering (GST)

Under GST law, any reduction in tax rates must be passed on to consumers through commensurate price reductions. We comply with the **anti-profiteering provisions** under GST.

### 12.3 No Dual MRP

- We do not engage in **dual MRP** practices.
- The MRP displayed on the product is the same as on the physical product packaging.
- Selling above MRP is illegal under the Legal Metrology Act, and we strictly comply with this requirement.
